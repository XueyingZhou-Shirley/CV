Xueying Zhou周雪盈

PhD Student in Musicology · Rutgers University 音乐学博士研究生 · 罗格斯大学

Xueying Zhou is a PhD student in Musicology at the Mason Gross School of the Arts, Rutgers University, where she is funded. Her research centers on the musical lives of Chinese diaspora communities in early twentieth-century North America — tracing Cantonese opera performances and Taishanese muk'yu songs in San Francisco from the 1880s to the 1940s. Drawing on archival research, music analysis, and cultural theory, she examines how sound became a site of cultural negotiation, memory, and belonging across borders. Her broader interests include music and gender, transcultural music studies, and the intersection of sonic practice with questions of identity and power.
